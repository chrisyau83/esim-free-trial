Quick fixes

Not connected yet?

Most first-connection issues are settings, not failed eSIMs.

No signal after arrival

Make sure the eSIM line is turned on and selected for mobile data. Toggle airplane mode for 30 seconds, then restart the phone if needed.

eSIM installs but data does not work

Check the provider's activation timing, APN, and data-roaming instructions. Turn off cellular data switching while testing the travel line.

The QR code will not scan

Increase screen brightness on the device displaying the code, clean the camera lens, or use the provider's manual activation details.

The phone says the code was already used

Many eSIM QR codes can be installed only once. Do not delete an installed eSIM unless the provider confirms it can be reissued.

Which provider offers a 1 GB eSIM Free Trial?

Will installing an eSIM remove my regular SIM?

No. Installing an eSIM adds another mobile plan. On dual-SIM phones, you can keep your primary line available for calls and texts while using the eSIM for data.

What happens after the 3-day eSIM Free Trial?

If you do not cancel before the end of day 3, Test Flight automatically continues at $9.99/month. Each paid renewal adds $9.99 in account credit that is valid for 30 days. You can cancel from your dashboard.

Do I need data roaming turned on?

Many travel eSIMs require roaming on the eSIM line. Follow the provider's instructions, and keep roaming off on your primary line if you want to avoid home-carrier charges.

Does the eSIM Free Trial include a phone number?

No. The trial is data-only. Your regular number stays on your main SIM, while apps such as WhatsApp, FaceTime, and iMessage can use the eSIM's data connection.

What happens if I cancel during the trial?

You pay nothing if you cancel within 3 days. The free test eSIM deactivates immediately when the Test Flight membership is cancelled.
